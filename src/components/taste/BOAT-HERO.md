# BoatHero

- Import: `import BoatHero from '../../components/taste/BoatHero.astro'` and `import BoatGlyph from '../../components/taste/BoatGlyph.astro'` (example: `src/pages/taste/boat-demo.astro`).
- `<BoatHero minHeight="100svh" class="my-hero">…your hero…</BoatHero>`. `minHeight` takes any CSS length and defaults to `100svh`. The section is a flex row with its content centred vertically, and your slot content gets full width.
- Slot: put everything there (headline, copy, buttons, the product example), laid out however you like (grid, two columns). BoatHero adds no padding or max-width on desktop.
- Put `<BoatGlyph />` directly after the last word of one sentence, inside the same element, with no space before it (it brings its own `&nbsp;`). It can sit in any column at any width. The script measures where it really is.
- The boat lands straight below the glyph, 37px down (30px under 768px), or on the front wave if that point would be above the water. It splashes, speeds up over 3s, then drifts at the live speed (25s per lap). Timing matches the live page.
- Tint by setting these on `.boat-hero` (or your `class`): `--boat-hero-bg` (section ground), `--boat-hero-sky` (sea backdrop, any `background`), `--boat-hero-wave-1/2/3` (wave fills), `--boat-hero-wave-opacity`, `--boat-hero-stars-opacity` (stars + grid), `--boat-hero-sea-height` (240px under 768px, 100% above). `--boat-hero-sink` (px, default 0) lands the boat that much deeper below the front wave's surface. The wave fills only apply in dark mode, because the site's `html.light` rules use `!important`.
- Under 768px the waves form a 240px band at the bottom and the content gets `padding-bottom: 0.72 × sea height`, so the boat lands below your stacked content. If you add your own bottom padding there, keep at least that much. The boat passes over the stacked card while it falls.
- Pointer events: the content layer lets clicks through to the boat (drag and toss work), except on links, buttons, form fields, headings, `p`, `li`, `pre`, `code` and `span`. A full-width element with an opaque background still covers the sea visually; it does not block clicks.
- Must not: put `overflow: hidden/clip` on an ancestor that is smaller than the hero (it clips the falling boat). Put `transform`, `filter`, `contain` or `will-change` on the section or `.boat-hero__sea` (that changes the boat's containing block and its landing maths). Give `.boat-hero__sea` a z-index (that traps the boat under the content). Raise your content above `z-index: 10`. Use more than one BoatHero per page (drag splash and toss height are page globals: `window._buoySplash`, `window._buoyBoatBottom`).
- z-index: sea 0, boat 5, content 10, glyph 20. The hero clips horizontally (`overflow: hidden`) so the drifting boat never causes horizontal scroll.
- Reduced motion: as on live. The glyph is hidden and the wave boat starts drifting straight away at its default spot (bottom 42%, left 15% of the sea).
- Don't edit `WaveBackground`/`WaveBoat`. BoatHero tints them from outside with scoped `:global()` rules.
